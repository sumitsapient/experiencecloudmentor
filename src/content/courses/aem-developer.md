A complete, hands-on journey through AEM as a Cloud Service — from your first component to architecture-level decisions on real enterprise projects.

Backed by 10+ years of AEM architecture experience across banking, financial services, and manufacturing, this course covers the same scenarios I've handled in production: scalable content models, system integrations, performance tuning, and the trade-off calls that separate a developer from an architect.

You won't just learn how AEM works — you'll learn how to build, deploy, and defend it in a real architecture review.

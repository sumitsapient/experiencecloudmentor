Edge Delivery Services (formerly Franklin/Helix) is Adobe's newest approach to building blazing-fast, content-first websites — and this course takes you from fundamentals to production-ready architecture.

With a strong AEM background and hands-on EDS project experience, I focus on what actually matters when shipping real sites: block-based authoring, Docs/Sheets-driven content modeling, and squeezing every bit of Core Web Vitals performance out of your pages.

By the end, you'll be able to build, deploy, and govern EDS projects — and know when EDS is the right fit versus traditional AEM.

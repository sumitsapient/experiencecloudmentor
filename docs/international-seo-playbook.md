# International SEO and Authority Playbook

Baseline from the October 1, 2026 GSC export: 808 impressions and 10 clicks across 12 active days. The United States produced 495 impressions at 0.20% CTR; India produced 87 impressions at 8.05% CTR. Treat early rankings as directional until each page has at least several weeks of stable data.

## Every 14 days

Export GSC Web performance data for Queries, Pages, Countries, and Devices. Compare the latest 14 days with the previous 14 days.

Prioritize pages using this order:

1. Positions 4-15 with meaningful impressions and low CTR.
2. Positions 16-30 with a close match between query and page intent.
3. Pages gaining impressions in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and Europe.
4. Mobile pages whose average position materially trails desktop.

For each updated page, record the date, query target, title change, content change, and expected result. Avoid changing the same title repeatedly before enough new data exists.

## Link-worthy assets

Promote assets that provide standalone technical value:

- Federated Audience Composition workflow diagram.
- AEP Edge Network routing diagram.
- Adobe product comparison tables.
- Certification study plans and checklists.
- Anonymized implementation case studies once factual details are approved.

Permit editorial reuse of original diagrams when the publisher links to the source article and credits ExperienceCloudMentor.

## Distribution workflow

For each substantial article update:

1. Publish one LinkedIn post containing the key answer or diagram, not only a link teaser.
2. Share the article in a relevant Adobe Experience League discussion when it directly answers the question.
3. Send it to Adobe-focused newsletter or community editors with a one-sentence explanation of the unique value.
4. Reference it from relevant GitHub examples or project documentation only when the article adds implementation context.
5. Revisit the article after 30 days and update weak sections using real query data.

## Outreach targets

Build a small, relevant list rather than buying placements:

- Adobe Experience League community contributors.
- Adobe Experience Cloud newsletters and independent blogs.
- AEM, AEP, MarTech, experimentation, and customer-data podcasts.
- Consultants writing about adjacent Adobe products.
- Technical training directories with editorial review.
- Conference and meetup organizers accepting practitioner sessions.

Reject paid link networks, bulk guest-post packages, unrelated directories, and reciprocal-link schemes.

## International conversion checks

Review course and service pages quarterly to ensure they clearly state:

- Worldwide remote delivery.
- Supported scheduling across international time zones.
- Response time and discovery process.
- Engagement deliverables.
- Payment and pricing information when approved for publication.
- Testimonials or case studies only when attributable and permitted.

Do not create country pages unless they contain genuinely distinct regional information. Avoid near-duplicate location pages.

## Success measures

Track outcomes by country and page:

- Non-India impressions and clicks.
- CTR for queries in positions 1-10 and 11-20.
- Number of priority pages entering the top 10.
- Referring domains from relevant technical sites.
- Course and consulting enquiries by country.
- Mobile versus desktop position and conversion rate.
